# Karigar Hisab — Orders, Labor, Attendance & Partner Ledger

A lightweight, mobile-friendly, multi-language web application for workshop owners, contractors, and small businesses to manage daily accounts effortlessly.

### Key Features
- **Order & Labor Billing:** Track work details, customer advances, GST, and pending dues.
- **Worker Attendance & Wages:** Daily and lumpsum (ઉચ્ચક) wage calculation with automatic attendance totals.
- **Partner & Worker Ledger:** Automatic expense deduction, profit-sharing, withdrawals (upad), and final settlement.
- **Customer & A4 Partner Print:** One-click printable bills and A4 partner hisab sheets, plus PDF & CSV export.
- **Sign in with Google Drive Backup:** Securely auto-save and restore your data in your own Google Drive, plus local JSON backup with Undo Reset protection.
- **Multi-Language Support:** Available in Gujarati, Hindi, English, Marathi, Punjabi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, and Urdu.
